# Structured Lead Card Schema

Use this markdown schema when presenting verified leads to the user and when appending to `.agents/career/job_leads.md`.

```markdown
#### [Job Title] — [Company Name]
- **Match Score**: **[XX]/100%** ([High Priority | Moderate | Disqualified])
- **Legitimacy Rating**: **[X]/5 Signal** ([Verified Opportunity | Review Needed | Suspicious])
- **Comp Confidence**: **[Tier 1: Stated Base $X | Tier 2: Stated Range | Tier 3: Unstated]**
- **Location / Overlap**: [Target Region & Timezone details]
- **Stack Alignment**: [Matched Tech Stack]
- **Why It Fits (STAR+R)**: [1-2 sentences highlighting alignment with candidate strengths and proven projects]
- **Potential Friction / Audit**: [Any experience gating, timezone issues, or stack deviations]
- **Verified Direct Permalink**: [Apply Here](URL)
```
