# Outreach & Pitch Templates

Select the format that best matches the channel and opportunity.

---

## 1. Async Founder DM / Cold Email (X, LinkedIn, Direct Email)
*For founders and hiring leads who appreciate concise, high-signal proof.*
- **Constraint**: Under 100 words. Active voice.
- **Structure**:
  1. **Hook**: Direct reference to the specific product feature or roadmap goal.
  2. **Proof-First Match**: State role alignment and cite matched project + quantified metric from `.agents/career/projects/`.
  3. **Call to Action**: Low-friction async chat or portfolio link.

---

## 2. Contract MVP Milestone Proposal
*For freelance builds, fractional engagements, or fixed-scope contract roles.*
- **Structure**:
  1. **Executive Summary**: Value proposition and rapid delivery timeline (e.g. 2–4 weeks).
  2. **Proposed Scope & Milestones**: Phase 1 Core Architecture, Phase 2 Auth/Data Flow, Phase 3 Polish & Deploy.
  3. **Tech Stack Contract**: Agreed framework, database, and hosting architecture.
  4. **STAR+R Proof Anchor**: Relevant case study with quantifiable outcome and reflection from `.agents/career/projects/`.

---

## 3. Direct ATS / Job Board Application Note
*For Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, WorkAtAStartup, and Indeed direct applications.*
- **Structure**:
  1. **Role & Intent**: Immediate clarity on target position and availability.
  2. **3 High-Impact Bullets**:
     - *Bullet 1*: Exact tech stack alignment.
     - *Bullet 2*: Direct domain proof point with quantified metric from `.agents/career/projects/`.
     - *Bullet 3*: Architecture & product delivery velocity.
  3. **Location & Work Authorization**: Work arrangements and timezone overlap.
